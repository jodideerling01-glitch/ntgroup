Investment Opportunities

Supporting informed, transparent and commercially sound decisions.

NT Group provides investors with access to structured mining opportunities across South Africa, Zimbabwe, and the broader SADC region.

We understand that serious investors require credible technical, geological, operational, and compliance information before making funding or partnership decisions.

As part of our mining investment linkage model, NT Group assists mine owners in preparing and presenting relevant mining documentation and technical data required for proper investor assessment and project evaluation.

Our goal is to help investors make informed, transparent, and commercially sound decisions based on verified project information and internationally recognised reporting standards.

Investor Support

Due diligence documentation and technical information.

Documentation depends on project stage, availability and the applicable legal or reporting framework.

Geological & Technical Reports

  • Geological reports
  • Mineral reserve reports
  • Exploration reports
  • Resource estimation reports
  • Desktop geological studies
  • Target detection reports
  • Remote sensing studies
  • Satellite mineral intelligence reports

International Reporting Standards

Where available, information may be presented in line with:

  • SAMREC / SAMVAL (South Africa)
  • JORC Code (Australia)
  • NI 43-101 (Canada / USA)

These standards help assess resource confidence, mineral potential, geological reliability, commercial viability and investment risk.

Exploration & Drilling Information

  • Drill hole information and number of holes
  • Core sample and assay data
  • Sample analysis reports
  • Geological mapping and trenching results
  • Topographical information
  • Depth and mineralisation data
  • Density and concentration analysis

Operational & Legal Documentation

  • Mining licences and registration documents
  • Claim ownership documentation
  • Environmental and regulatory records
  • Production records
  • Equipment and operational assessments
  • Mine development plans
  • Partnership and structuring proposals
