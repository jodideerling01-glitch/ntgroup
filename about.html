About NT Group

Unlocking mine value through linkage, intelligence and production support.

NT Group is a strategic mining investment linkage and production-scaling company focused on supporting mining operations across South Africa, Zimbabwe, and the broader SADC region.

We specialise in helping mining projects unlock value through investment facilitation, machinery partnerships, production optimisation, mineral intelligence, and strategic stakeholder coordination.

Our business model is built around sustainable growth, responsible mining development, and mutually beneficial partnerships that protect both mine owners and investors.

Our Vision

To become Africa’s leading mining linkage and production-scaling partner.

Creating sustainable mining partnerships that unlock economic growth across the continent.

Our Mission

To connect opportunity with capital, technology, equipment and markets.

We protect mine ownership while promoting responsible resource development.
