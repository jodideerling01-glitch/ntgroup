NT GROUP WEBSITE — FILE GUIDE

OPENING THE WEBSITE
1. Keep all files and folders together.
2. Double-click index.html to open the Home page.
3. Use the menu to move between the eight separate pages.

PAGES
- index.html — Home
- about.html — About Us
- services.html — Services
- how-it-works.html — How It Works
- investors.html — Investors
- technology.html — Technology
- impact.html — Impact
- contact.html — Contact Us

NAVIGATION STRUCTURE
Five top-level items are visible:
Home | About Us | Services | Investors | Contact Us

The Services dropdown contains:
Services Overview | How It Works | Technology | Impact

EDITING
- Website wording: edit the relevant .html page.
- Colours and layout: css/styles.css
- Menu and form behaviour: js/script.js
- Images: assets/images
- Logos: assets/logo

CONTACT FORM
The static contact form validates required fields and opens the visitor's email application with the enquiry addressed to info@ntgroup.co.za.

SOURCE CONTENT
The supplied Word document is stored in source/NT Group Web content.docx.
The supplied logo source is stored in source/NT GROUP LOGO VECTOR.pdf.

PUBLISHING
Upload the full folder contents to your web host's public_html or www folder. index.html must remain at the root.
